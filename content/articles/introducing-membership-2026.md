Mwmbl is introducing membership, an important step toward supporting
the continued development of its open-source search engine.

Membership offers a way for people who believe in independent search
to support the project and contribute to its future.

## Why membership?

Running a search engine takes infrastructure, computing resources,
and ongoing development. Membership can help support these needs
while allowing Mwmbl to continue improving its search experience.

It also gives the community a way to get involved in supporting an
alternative to traditional search engines.

## Supporting independent search

Mwmbl aims to provide an alternative to established search engines
through open-source development and community involvement.

Building and maintaining a search index requires ongoing work, from
crawling the web to improving search results. Support from members
can help sustain these efforts and strengthen the project's future.

## Membership benefits

Membership may offer different benefits depending on the level of
support. For the latest information about membership options and
benefits, see Mwmbl's official membership information.

## What's next?

Membership is an opportunity to build a stronger community around
Mwmbl and support its long-term development.

With continued community support, Mwmbl can keep working toward
making independent search a useful alternative for everyone.

## Finally...

Thank you to everyone who supports open-source search and the
development of Mwmbl. Community support makes a difference.

If you're interested in the project, visit the
