Mwmbl is continuing to develop its search engine with new features and ways for the community to get involved. One of these features is Seed Search, which helps improve search results while expanding Mwmbl's own search index.

## What is Seed Search?

Seed Search combines Mwmbl's existing search index with results from external search providers. It helps Mwmbl discover pages that its own index might not have found yet.

Mwmbl is an independent, open-source, non-profit search engine. Because its index is smaller than those of major commercial search engines, it can sometimes miss pages that users are looking for. Seed Search is designed to help address this problem.

## How does Seed Search work?

Seed Search works through several steps:

1. **Search:** Mwmbl searches its own index and uses external search results to find additional pages.
2. **Collect results:** Results from the different sources are gathered together.
3. **Rank results:** The results are scored and ordered to help users find relevant pages.
4. **Grow the index:** Newly discovered pages can help expand Mwmbl's own index and improve future searches.

This means Seed Search can help users find pages while also helping Mwmbl improve its search index over time.

## Membership and quotas

Mwmbl has membership tiers that offer different levels of access to Seed Search and other features.

The proposed membership allowances include:

- **Sprout:** 300 Seed Search queries per month.
- **Sapling:** 1,500 Seed Search queries per month.
- **Canopy:** Includes Sapling benefits, plus up to one million pages crawled per month against the member's username.

These tiers provide different levels of access while helping manage the resources needed to operate the service. The crawling allowance in Canopy also gives members an additional way to contribute to the growth of Mwmbl's index.

Membership benefits and quotas may change as Mwmbl continues development, so check the current membership details before publishing these figures.

## Privacy

Mwmbl is focused on building independent search technology. When Seed Search uses external providers, query text may be sent to those providers to retrieve or rank results.

Users should check Mwmbl's current documentation for the details of how queries are processed and what information is shared with external services.

## What's next?

Seed Search is part of Mwmbl's ongoing work to improve search quality and expand its independent index. By combining its own search capabilities with external results, Mwmbl can discover more pages and make them available to future searches.

As the project develops, the functionality, quotas, and membership benefits may continue to change.

## Finally...

Mwmbl continues to explore ways to make independent search more useful while keeping its technology open source. Seed Search is one example of how search features can help users find pages today while contributing to a better index for tomorrow.
